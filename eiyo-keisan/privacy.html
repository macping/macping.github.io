English

This app works fully offline and does not collect or transmit any personal data.

  • All data you enter (menus, recipes, foods) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • No account is required, and no ads are shown.
  • In-app purchases (one-time Pro unlock) are processed by Google Play / the App Store; the developer never receives your payment details.
  • CSV/Excel files leave your device only when you explicitly share or export them.
