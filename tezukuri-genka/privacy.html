English

"Craft Pricing Calculator" (the App) is provided by an independent developer.

Data we collect

The App itself requires no account and does not collect or transmit personal information such as your name or email address. Your materials, works, costs, prices and settings are stored only on your device. The App works fully offline.

Backups

The optional JSON backup is a file you export and keep yourself (e.g. via the share sheet). It is never uploaded by the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID, approximate location and device information to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and Ads Policy. After the one-time "remove ads" purchase, no ads are shown and no AdMob collection occurs.

In-app purchase

The one-time ad-removal purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. The developer never receives your payment details.

Contact: macping@gmail.com
