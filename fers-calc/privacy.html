English

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • All calculations run entirely on your device. Your salary, service history, TSP balance, and every other input stay local and are never sent anywhere.
  • No account is required and none can be created.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Google Play / Apple; we receive no personal information from it. Purchase state is stored only on your device.
  • Links to official sources (OPM.gov, SSA.gov) open in your browser; the app itself makes no network requests.
  • No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page before the change takes effect.

Contact: macping@gmail.com
