English

Summary: this app sends nothing to the developer or to any third party. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device.

  • Fully offline: All payroll calculation and payslip generation happens on the device. There is no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Data stored on the device: Employer settings, employee records (name, date of birth, address, hire date, wages, number of dependents, insurance enrollment flags, resident-tax amounts, etc.) and generated payslips are kept in a JSON file inside the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it and the developer has no access to it.
  • My Number: The app has no field for the Japanese Individual Number (My Number). Do not enter it anywhere in the app.
  • Employer responsibility: The employer who uses the app is the controller of the employees' personal data stored on the device and is responsible for handling it lawfully. The app only helps the employer keep their own records; the developer does not receive or hold any of it.
  • Actions that leave the device (all started by you):
    • Sharing a PDF / CSV / JSON backup: the OS share sheet opens and you choose the destination. The destination's own policy applies.
    • Opening the app's Google Play page.
    • Sending feedback: your own mail app opens; you decide whether and what to send.
  • Automatic network access: The only connection the app makes on its own is a read-only check for app updates (Google Play in-app update API on Android, Apple iTunes lookup API on iOS). No personal data is sent.
  • In-app purchase: The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Google Play / Apple App Store under their privacy policies. The developer never sees payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.
  • Deleting data: Deleting data inside the app or uninstalling the app removes everything from the device. The developer holds nothing, so there is nothing to request deletion of.

摘要：本应用不向开发者或任何第三方发送数据。您输入的全部内容仅保存在您设备上应用的私有存储中。

  • 保存在设备上的数据： 事业所设置、员工信息（姓名、出生日期、住址、入职日期、工资、扶养人数、社会保险加入状态、住民税金额等）以及生成的工资单，均保存在应用私有存储内的一个 JSON 文件中。其他应用无法读取，开发者也无法访问。
  • 个人编号（My Number）： 本应用没有个人编号输入栏，请勿在任何位置输入。
  • 雇主责任： 保存在设备上的员工个人信息由使用本应用的雇主自行管理并承担责任。本应用仅帮助雇主整理自己的账簿，开发者不获取、不保存任何内容。
  • 会离开设备的操作（均由您本人发起）：
    • 分享 PDF / CSV / JSON 备份：系统分享面板打开，由您选择目的地，并适用该目的地的隐私政策。
    • 打开本应用的 Google Play 页面。
    • 发送反馈：打开您自己的邮件应用，是否发送、发送什么由您决定。
  • 应用自动发起的网络访问： 仅有一项，即只读的应用更新检查（Android 使用 Google Play 应用内更新 API，iOS 使用 Apple iTunes lookup API）。不发送任何个人数据。
