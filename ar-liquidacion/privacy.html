Esta aplicación no recopila ni transmite datos personales.

English

This app does not collect or transmit personal data.

  • All calculations run on your phone, offline. Dates, salaries, the optional name and CUIL you type never leave the device; name and CUIL are only printed in the PDF you generate.
  • No account or registration is required.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect the device advertising ID and technical device data under Google's policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can withdraw consent through the privacy form the app shows. The one-time "Pro" purchase removes all ads.
  • Billing is handled by Google Play; the app never accesses your payment details.
  • At start-up the app asks the store whether a newer version exists (read-only, no data sent).
  • The calculation history is stored on your phone only; uninstalling deletes everything. No analytics or crash-reporting services are used.

Unofficial app — no connection with the Secretaría de Trabajo, ARCA or any State body. Results are indicative estimates, not legal advice.
